What is Epiandrosterone?

Epiandrosterone, more popularly known as Epi Andro or 3β-androsterone, is a metabolic derivative of Dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA) and testosterone (1).

DHEA, an adrenal hormone produced in the body, is converted into Epiandrosterone by the activity of an enzyme called 5α-reductase.

When you take Epiandrosterone, it immediately converts itself into Dihydrotestosterone (DHT). And, for this reason, it is also referred to as a prohormone.

Because it is a prohormone, Epi Andro has weak androgenic activity compared to its biochemical products.
