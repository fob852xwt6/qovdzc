How is pain radiating from the buttock to the thigh typically treated?

Pain radiating from the buttock to the thigh is often caused by nerve irritation, muscle tightness, or joint dysfunction. Effective treatments typically include physical therapy to improve mobility and strength, dry needling to release trigger points, and targeted care to address specific nerve involvement such as sciatica or piriformis syndrome. Because the underlying cause can vary widely, from a pinched nerve to referred pain from the lower back or hip, identifying the source is essential for effective relief.
