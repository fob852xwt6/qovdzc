How Semaglutide Works in the Body

To understand why nutrition matters so much while using semaglutide, it helps to know how the medication works.

Semaglutide mimics a naturally occurring hormone (GLP-1) that:

Delays stomach emptying, helping you feel fuller for longer

Reduces appetite by acting on hunger-regulating centers in the brain

Lowers blood sugar by enhancing insulin release (1)

This combination helps drive weight loss, but it also reduces how much food and nutrition you take in. If you’re not careful, you could lose muscle along with fat or end up dragging through your day with low energy.That’s where a dialed-in eating plan comes in. Protein-heavy diets have been shown to help older men retain lean mass and reduce fat mass during attempted weight loss periods. (2)

Foods to Limit or Avoid

Alternatively, there are a variety of foods that simply don’t play well when developing a semaglutide meal plan. Eating the wrong foods while taking semaglutide for weight loss can worsen side effects and stall overall progress.Some foods to stay away from include:

High-fat or fried foods – High-fat foods can increase side effects of semaglutide such as indigestion, nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea since the food is sitting in your stomach for a longer period of time. (5)

Sugary snacks and desserts – Eating sugary snacks and desserts while on semaglutide can cause blood sugar spikes followed by crashes that will leave you drained.

Carbonated beverages – Sodas and other carbonated drinks can cause an increase of bloating and gas when your stomach empties more slowly.

Alcohol – Alcohol can interfere with blood sugar regulation for semaglutide patients, and make side effects like dizziness worse. The good news is that studies have shown that semaglutide decreases alcohol cravings and drinking quantity for adults with symptoms of alcohol use disorder. (6)

When to Adjust Your Diet

Semaglutide affects everyone differently, and sometimes adjustments are needed. If you start experiencing unwanted symptoms or hit a plateau, your nutrition may need a tweak.

If you’re feeling fatigued or weak, add in complex carbs such as oats or sweet potatoes or increase protein to support lean body mass and energy levels.

If you’re experiencing nausea, try having smaller, low-fat meals at frequent intervals throughout the day. Avoid greasy or rich foods, as they can enhance nausea and other stomach issues.

If you begin to notice muscle loss while on semaglutide, it may be time to start or increase a resistance training program to preserve lean muscle mass. Additionally, make sure you’re carefully tracking and hitting your protein goals, as a lack of protein could be causing a decrease in muscle mass.
