Explanation

Possible causes of a swollen and painful forearm include:

Injury or trauma: Sprains, strains, fractures, or overuse injuries can cause swelling and pain. Infections: Skin infections such as cellulitis or abscesses may cause redness, warmth, swelling, and pain. Tendonitis or tenosynovitis: Inflammation of tendons can cause pain and swelling. Blood clots (deep vein thrombosis): A clot in the veins of the forearm can cause sudden swelling and pain. Lymphedema: Fluid buildup from lymphatic blockage can cause swelling and discomfort. Compartment syndrome: A rare but serious condition where pressure builds in muscle compartments, causing severe pain and swelling.
