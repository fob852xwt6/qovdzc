Explanation

Patchy hair loss, commonly known as alopecia areata, is a condition where the body's immune system attacks hair follicles, leading to hair loss. Several treatments can help manage this condition.

Common Treatments:

Corticosteroids: These are often used to reduce inflammation and suppress the immune system. They can be applied as creams, injected, or taken as pills.

Minoxidil: This topical solution can help stimulate hair growth and is often used alongside other treatments.

JAK Inhibitors: These newer medications target specific parts of the immune system and have shown promise in treating alopecia areata.
