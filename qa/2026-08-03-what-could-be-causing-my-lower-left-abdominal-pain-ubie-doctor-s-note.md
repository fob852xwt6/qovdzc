Explanation

Lower left abdominal pain can have a number of causes. Some common reasons include digestive problems like constipation or diverticulitis, which is an inflammation of small pouches in the colon. Other possible causes are infections, such as a urinary tract infection, or issues with reproductive organs in women, like ovarian cysts.

Common Causes:

Digestive Issues: Problems like constipation or diverticulitis.

Infections: Such as a urinary tract infection.
