# In the '90s, JFK Jr. Was a Style Icon. Here's How to Nail His Signature Look Today.

WITH RYAN MURPHY’S new Love Story—starring Paul Anthony Kelly as John F. Kennedy Jr.—putting JFK Jr. back in the spotlight, it’s a good time to revisit why his style still works. The guy could really dress—and not in a runway, fashion-week way. His look was classic, relaxed, and looks flattering on pretty much any guy. Navy suits that fit. Straight-leg dark jeans. White tees. Trim sweaters. Blazers that made him look sharp without looking overdressed. Nothing loud. Nothing complicated.

It’s the same formula you’re seeing on guys like Jacob Elordi, whether he’s hitting the red carpet or just walking down the street. And it’s one of the easiest wardrobes to shop for right now. I wear some version of it to the office, out to dinner, and on weekends when I’m hanging with friends. If you don’t “get” fashion, even better. JFK Jr.’s whole thing was simplicity. Let’s break it down.

The Tailoring: Clean, Not Flashy

When John F. Kennedy Jr. put on a suit, he didn't overdo it. It was simple: navy, charcoal, maybe a deep midnight. The fit was tailored without being tight. Structured shoulders on the blazer, clean drape through the body, and trousers with a natural line. Add a crisp white or pale blue dress shirt and a simple silk tie. Black or dark brown cap-toe Oxfords.

JFK Jr. understood business casual before it was a corporate buzzword. A navy blazer over a minimal sweater. A soft Oxford button-down tucked into tailored chinos. Loafers or simple derbies. The palette stays neutral—khaki, grey, navy, forest green—so everything mixes and matches. This is the formula I lean on during the week: sharp enough for meetings, easy enough that you don’t feel overdressed at happy hour.

The Jeans-and-Sweater Formula

JFK Jr. also knew how to dress things down. Think: Dark, straight-leg jeans, a white crewneck tee—maybe a navy cardigan or a trim cashmere sweater. Clean white sneakers or broken-in leather boots. Nothing was distressing, oversized, or splashed with logos across your chest. The vibe is simple and fresh.

Outerwear That Finishes the Look

Think timeless staples: a tailored topcoat, a suede bomber, a classic field jacket. Nothing overly technical like a puffer jacket. The outer layer should be sharp and elevate the rest of the outfit.
