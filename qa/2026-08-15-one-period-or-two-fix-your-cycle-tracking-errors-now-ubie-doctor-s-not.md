Tracking your menstrual cycle can help clarify whether you're experiencing one period or two. A typical cycle lasts 21–35 days, with bleeding for 2–7 days. If bleeding occurs twice within a single month, it may indicate two separate periods, hormonal changes, or spotting between cycles. Factors like stress, birth control, thyroid issues, or perimenopause can also affect frequency. Keeping a log of start dates, flow, and symptoms helps you and your doctor identify patterns.
