Many women experience pain during intercourse, which is called dyspareunia. This pain can happen for different reasons, such as infections, hormonal changes, or other medical conditions.

What to Do:

See a Doctor: It is important to talk to a healthcare provider to figure out why the pain is happening. They can help find the cause and suggest treatments.

Treatment Options: Depending on the cause, treatments may include medication, physical therapy, or counseling. Sometimes, lifestyle changes or exercises can help too.

Self-Care: Simple things like using lubrication or changing positions might help reduce pain.

If you have pain during intercourse, don't ignore it. Getting help can make a big difference in feeling better and enjoying intimacy.
