Probiotics are live bacteria that are good for your gut health. They can help treat diarrhea, especially when it is caused by infections. Research shows that probiotics can shorten the duration of diarrhea and make it less severe.

How Probiotics Help:

Restoring Balance: When you have diarrhea, the balance of good and bad bacteria in your gut is upset. Probiotics help restore this balance by adding more good bacteria.

Fighting Infections: Some probiotics can fight off the bad bacteria or viruses causing the diarrhea.

Types of Diarrhea:

Acute Infectious Diarrhea: Probiotics are particularly effective in treating diarrhea caused by infections, like from viruses or bacteria.

Antibiotic-Associated Diarrhea: They can also help when diarrhea happens after taking antibiotics, which can kill both good and bad bacteria.

Overall, using probiotics can be a safe and helpful way to manage diarrhea, but if you're unsure about your symptoms, you can check your symptoms with a free AI symptom checker before consulting with a healthcare provider about treatment options.
