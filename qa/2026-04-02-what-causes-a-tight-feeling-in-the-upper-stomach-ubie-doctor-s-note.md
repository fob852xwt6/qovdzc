Explanation

Feeling tightness in the upper stomach can be uncomfortable and concerning. There are a few potential causes for this sensation:

Abdominal Wall Pain: This type of pain comes from the muscles and tissues in the abdominal wall itself. It can feel like tightness or cramping. This type of pain is often overlooked but is a common cause of discomfort in the abdominal area.

Gallstone Disease: Gallstones are hard particles that form in the gallbladder. They can cause a tight feeling, especially after eating fatty foods, because they block the flow of bile, leading to pressure and discomfort.

Celiac Plexus Syndrome: This is a condition related to the celiac plexus, a network of nerves near the stomach. Issues with this nerve bundle can cause a tight or painful feeling in the upper abdomen.

If you are experiencing persistent or severe discomfort, it is important to consult with a healthcare provider for a proper diagnosis and treatment plan.
