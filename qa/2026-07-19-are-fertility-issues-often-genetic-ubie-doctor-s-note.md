Explanation

Fertility issues can indeed have a genetic basis, as many cases are linked to mutations in genes that play vital roles in reproduction. Research indicates that loss-of-function mutations can disrupt normal reproductive processes, leading to infertility.

Key Points:

Genetic Mutations: Specific changes in genes can lead to problems with fertility. These mutations may affect how reproductive organs develop or function.

Research Findings: Studies have shown that understanding these genetic factors is crucial for diagnosing and potentially treating infertility.

Ongoing Studies: Scientists are continually exploring how genetic information can be used to better understand and address fertility issues.
