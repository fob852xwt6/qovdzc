What is NAD therapy?

NAD (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme that is essential to cellular energy function in the human body. NAD levels fall naturally as people age, resulting in lower levels of metabolic energy. NAD therapy is designed to help replenish NAD levels and improve NAD’s regenerative effects.

The damage that occurs to brain cells over time impacts their adaptability to changing environments – known as plasticity. It also reduces their neuronal stress resistance.
