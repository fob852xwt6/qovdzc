Sciatica is not directly caused by lifestyle factors, but certain habits can significantly increase your risk. Poor posture, a sedentary lifestyle, lack of regular exercise, and obesity all place added stress on the lower spine and sciatic nerve. Over time, this pressure can lead to nerve irritation, muscle imbalances, and disc problems that trigger sciatica symptoms.

Because sciatica pain can range from mild tingling to debilitating discomfort—and because its underlying causes vary widely—it's important to understand what may be driving your symptoms before deciding on next steps. Explanation

Sciatica is a condition that involves pain along the sciatic nerve, which runs from the lower back down through the legs. While lifestyle factors such as poor posture, lack of exercise, and obesity can contribute to the development of sciatica, they are not considered direct causes.

Factors Contributing to Sciatica:

Poor Posture: Sitting or standing improperly can put extra pressure on the spine and nerves, potentially leading to sciatica.

Lack of Exercise: Not staying active can weaken muscles that support the spine, increasing the risk of nerve compression.

Obesity: Excess weight can place additional stress on the lower back, heightening the chance of developing sciatica.
