Pain during sex, also known as dyspareunia, can be influenced by various factors. Increasing sexual arousal may help reduce the intensity of pain experienced during intercourse. This suggests that lifestyle changes that enhance sexual arousal could be beneficial.

To address pain, consider the following lifestyle changes:

Focus on Arousal: Enhancing sexual arousal before and during sex can potentially decrease pain. This may involve spending more time on foreplay or engaging in activities that increase comfort and relaxation.

Improve Sexual Satisfaction: Ensuring that both partners feel satisfied and comfortable can also help. Open communication about likes, dislikes, and boundaries can lead to a more satisfying experience.

Address Self-Discrepancies: Feeling comfortable and confident in one's sexual identity and body can improve satisfaction and potentially reduce pain.
