Explanation

Sometimes, your one eye might feel like it’s on fire and start watering a lot. This can happen when the eye is irritated. One reason could be dry eye disease. It sounds weird, but when your eye doesn’t have enough moisture, it tries to protect itself by making extra tears, which can cause both burning and watering. Another possible reason is an infection, like viral conjunctivitis (sometimes called pink eye). This infection can make your eye red, sore, and watery. Sinusitis, or an infection of the sinuses, might also affect just one eye if it spreads or causes pressure in that area. Even COVID-19 has been linked with burning, watery eyes for some people. Each of these issues can cause discomfort and might need treatments like eye drops, proper cleaning, or sometimes more specific medications. If your one eye keeps burning and watering, it’s important to talk with an eye doctor or healthcare professional. They can give you a proper exam and help decide the best way to make your eye feel better.
