# Q: Can I Wear Linen After Labor Day?

My job recently relocated me from Chicago to Los Angeles, and while I really love the lifestyle and the weather, I feel thoroughly confused about what to wear. For instance, I was wondering if I can still wear linen after Labor Day? It's so hot and sunny here still, it feels okay, but you're the expert and I wanted your opinion. White pants in winter? Should I tuck my cotton khaki suit away until next year, or is it okay to wear now?

Dear Brad,

First of all, congratulations on your move from the Windy City to the City of Angels. Just in time to escape the cold. The one drawback to moving from a 4-season climate to one that’s pretty temperate or even warm year round is sartorial confusion.

A good rule to remember is that there are no rules anymore. Or should we say, the iron-clad rules of the past have eased up and you are encouraged to abide by the temperatures rather than the calendar. So wear your khaki suit, your linen trousers and even your white jeans (see how Nautica’s creative director Chris Cox rocks them without shame after Labor Day, here). As long as you feel comfortable and the sun is in your favor, take a cue from the natives.

In general, if you move from Boston to Tuscan, don’t feel obligated to wear gray tweeds and wool just because it’s November. In fact, take advantage of the laid-back temps and embrace spring and summer colors like these year round. Leave the parkas, wooly scarves and heavy jackets to the rest of us shivering in the cold.

Good luck!
