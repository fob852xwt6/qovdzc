Changes in poop smell are a normal part of digestion and can be influenced by several factors. When food moves through your digestive system, different compounds are broken down by gut bacteria, creating various odors. The type and amount of protein, fiber, and other nutrients in your food can all affect how your stool smells. Sometimes changes in smell can also happen when you take certain medications or supplements, as these can affect how your body processes food and the way your gut bacteria work. Most of the time, this is not a cause for concern. However, if the change persists or is accompanied by other concerning symptoms like pain, you should discuss with a healthcare provider.
