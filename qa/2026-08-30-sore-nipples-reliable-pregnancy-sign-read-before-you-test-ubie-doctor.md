Are sore nipples a reliable sign of pregnancy?

[Doctor's Note](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/3444554038.html)

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Sore nipples can be an early sign of pregnancy, often caused by hormonal shifts that increase blood flow and breast sensitivity. However, sore nipples alone are not a reliable indicator of pregnancy, as they also commonly occur with premenstrual syndrome (PMS), hormonal fluctuations, breastfeeding, friction, or skin conditions.

Because nipple soreness overlaps with so many possible causes, the best next step is to look at your full picture of symptoms rather than one clue in isolation.
