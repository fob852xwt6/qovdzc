How common is obesity worldwide?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Obesity is one of the most common chronic health conditions worldwide, affecting hundreds of millions of people across nearly every country. Defined as excess body fat that increases the risk of serious health problems, obesity is linked to type 2 diabetes, heart disease, stroke, and certain cancers. Its causes are complex, involving genetics, diet, physical activity, environment, and underlying medical conditions.

Because obesity often overlaps with other symptoms — fatigue, joint pain, hormonal changes, or shortness of breath — it can be difficult to know whether what you're experiencing is related to weight, another condition, or both.
