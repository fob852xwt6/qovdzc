# ​Hate Laundry? This Deodorizing Hanger Keeps Your Clothes Fresh

If you dread laundry day or if you're simply looking for an easier way to keep your clothes gym-stench-free, you're in luck. Panasonic just released a new deodorizing hanger, which is your best new excuse for not completely avoiding the washing machine for days.

Similar to this gym bag, the deodorizing hanger destroys unpleasant odors attached to clothing, which means large amounts of water or detergent aren't necessary. If you go to gym daily, you probably go through clothing quickly. If you have a few go-to gym outfits, you can pick them out at the beginning of the week, deodorize them after an intense workout and reduce the amount of sweat-ridden clothing at the end of the week.

[Hanger Keeps Your Clothes](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/)

The secret to the hanger's hygienic abilities is something called "nanoe" technology, which refers to the use of electrified microscopic water particles that circulate and refresh your clothing. The hanger also makes it easier to keep a small number of clothing items fresh, so you can add this to your list of ways to defunk your smelly gym clothes. In addition to sweat, the hanger also has the ability to tackle the stench of tobacco and grilled meat (though anyone who smells like a ribeye is all right in our book).

The hanger comes equipped with two operational modes. The first is normal mode, which takes five hours for the clothes to deodorize. The second takes seven hours, and is meant for smells that are more difficult to extract. Panasonic's hanger also comes with a clothing cover to get rid of tougher, set-in odors.

According to the press release, the hanger is planned to be released September 1. The only catch is it might only be available in Japan initially and will be priced at 20,000 yen ($180), according to Kaden. While the hanger shouldn't be used in place of a washer and dryer entirely, if you truly despise doing laundry, getting your hands on one of these might be worth the trip.
