Explanation

Pain in the right testicle can happen for different reasons. One cause is a varicocele, which is a collection of veins around the testicle. This can cause discomfort and may need treatment to feel better.

Another possible cause is testicular torsion. This happens when the testicle twists, cutting off its blood supply. It is a serious condition that needs emergent medical help. Sometimes, doctors can untwist the testicle with a procedure called manual detorsion. Other causes include epididymitis and hernias, both of which also require medical evaluation and treatment.

If you have testicular pain, it is important to see a doctor. They can find out what is causing the pain and help you get the right treatment. Remember, getting help quickly can make a big difference, especially if it is something serious like testicular torsion.
