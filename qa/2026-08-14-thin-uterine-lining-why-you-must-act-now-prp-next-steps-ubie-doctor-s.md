Why is your uterine lining thin? PRP and medical next steps

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A thin uterine lining—typically under 7 mm—can result from hormonal imbalances, uterine scarring, reduced blood flow, or side effects from certain medications. This condition may contribute to irregular periods, failed embryo implantation, or miscarriage. Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy is an emerging treatment that involves injecting your own growth-factor-rich platelets into the uterine cavity. Combined with hormone optimization and targeted procedures, PRP can help improve endometrial thickness and receptivity, supporting better reproductive outcomes.

Because thin uterine lining shares symptoms with many other reproductive and hormonal conditions, identifying the root cause early is essential. Explanation

Understanding Thin Uterine Lining

A healthy uterine lining (endometrium) is crucial for menstruation and pregnancy. When it's too thin—generally under 7 mm on ultrasound—it can lead to irregular periods, difficulty conceiving, or challenges in embryo implantation during IVF.

Common Causes

Hormonal imbalances– Low estrogen levels (perimenopause, poor ovarian reserve)– Luteal phase defects (insufficient progesterone)

Uterine scarring or Asherman's syndrome– From repeated dilation and curettage (D&C) or infections

Prior surgeries– Myomectomy, cesarean section scars

Medications– Long-term use of certain hormonal contraceptives

Chronic inflammation or infections– Endometritis can thin the lining over time

Poor blood flow– Fibroids or polyps blocking uterine vessels

Why Thin Lining Matters for Fertility

Implantation issues: A thinner lining may not support embryo attachment.

Higher miscarriage risk: Insufficient endometrial development can increase early pregnancy loss.

Cycle irregularities: Light or absent periods often coincide with inadequate lining growth.

How It's Evaluated

Transvaginal ultrasound: Measures thickness and checks for scarring or fibroids.

Hysteroscopy: Direct visualization if scarring or polyps are suspected.

Blood tests: Estrogen, progesterone, thyroid hormones, and prolactin levels.

Endometrial biopsy: Rarely used, but can rule out chronic inflammation or cell abnormalities.

PRP for Thin Uterine Lining

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy is gaining attention as a way to improve endometrial thickness and receptivity.

What Is PRP?

A concentrate of your own platelets, drawn from a small blood sample.

Platelets release growth factors that promote tissue repair and blood vessel formation.

How PRP Is Used in Fertility

Blood is drawn and spun in a centrifuge to isolate platelets.

Under ultrasound guidance, PRP is infused directly into the uterine cavity.

Generally done during the proliferative phase (just after menstruation).

Often repeated over 2–3 cycles if needed.

Evidence and Outcomes

Small clinical trials and case series report thicker linings (increase of 1–2 mm) and improved implantation rates.

A 2020 study showed over 60% of participants who previously failed IVF cycles achieved a lining >7 mm after PRP.

Larger, randomized trials are still under way to confirm efficacy and optimal protocols.

Potential Benefits

Autologous treatment: Uses your own blood, reducing risk of allergic reaction.

Minimally invasive: Quick outpatient procedure with minimal discomfort.

Considerations and Risks

Mild cramping or spotting may occur after infusion.

Infection risk is very low when performed under sterile conditions.

Cost and accessibility: Not all fertility centers offer PRP; insurance may not cover it.

Not a guaranteed fix: Works best when combined with hormone optimization.

Other Medical Next Steps

PRP can be one part of a broader strategy to optimize your uterine lining.

1. Hormonal Optimization

Estrogen supplementation: Oral, transdermal, or vaginal estrogens can help thicken the lining.

Progesterone support: Essential in the luteal phase (vaginal gels, suppositories, or oral).

Clomiphene citrate or letrozole: Stimulate endogenous estrogen in ovulatory cycles.

2. Uterine Procedures

Hysteroscopic adhesiolysis: Breaks down scar tissue in Asherman's syndrome.

Fibroid or polyp removal: Improves blood flow and space for lining growth.

3. Adjunctive Therapies

Low-dose aspirin or heparin: May improve blood flow in select cases.

Pentoxifylline and vitamin E: Some small studies suggest they aid endometrial regeneration.

Granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (G-CSF): Investigational; may enhance lining thickness.

4. Lifestyle and Supportive Measures

Nutrition: Adequate protein and essential fatty acids support hormone production.

Stress management: High stress can impact cycles; consider mindfulness or counseling.

Low-impact exercise: Promotes circulation without compromising pelvic blood flow.

When to Seek Further Evaluation

Even with PRP and medical therapies, persistent thin lining warrants deeper investigation:

Persistent thickness <6 mm after 2–3 optimized cycles.

Recurrent implantation failure in IVF (≥3 transfers of good-quality embryos).

Talking with Your Doctor

Always share your full medical history, including:

Prior surgeries or procedures

Medication and supplement use

Laboratory and imaging results

Discuss the pros and cons of PRP in the context of your individual fertility plan. Ask about:

Success rates at your clinic

Number of PRP treatments recommended

Combined protocols (hormones + adjunctive therapies)

If you experience any alarming signs—heavy bleeding, severe pain, fever, or signs of infection—speak to a doctor immediately.

Final Thoughts

A thin uterine lining can be challenging, but modern approaches like PRP therapy, hormone optimization, and targeted procedures offer hope. By combining evidence-based treatments with lifestyle support, many women see improved endometrial health and better fertility outcomes.

Always consult your healthcare professional before starting any new therapy. If you have life-threatening or serious symptoms, seek immediate medical attention. Every case is unique, so personalized care is key to finding the right solution for you.
