What is a mood swings disorder and how is it diagnosed?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Mood swings disorder is characterized by significant, extreme shifts in mood and is most commonly associated with bipolar disorder. Diagnosis typically involves a healthcare provider observing and evaluating these mood patterns over time, rather than relying on a single test.

Key facts about mood swings disorder:

Symptoms: Rapid or intense shifts between emotional highs (mania or hypomania) and lows (depression).

Diagnosis

History: A detailed medical and family history is taken to see if there are any patterns or triggers.
