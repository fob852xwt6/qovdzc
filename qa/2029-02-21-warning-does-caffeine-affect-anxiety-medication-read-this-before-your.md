Published on: 7/15/2026

Can caffeine affect anxiety medication?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Caffeine can interact with anxiety medications and may worsen anxiety symptoms. Common interactions include:

Reduced medication effectiveness: Caffeine can interfere with how anxiety medications like SSRIs and benzodiazepines work.

Increased side effects: Combining caffeine with anxiety medications may heighten jitteriness, restlessness, and insomnia.

Elevated heart rate and blood pressure: Both caffeine and certain anxiety medications can affect cardiovascular function.

Worsened anxiety symptoms: Caffeine stimulates the nervous system, potentially triggering or intensifying anxiety.

Explanation

Caffeine is a common stimulant found in coffee, tea, and many soft drinks. It can affect how anxiety medications work. Caffeine can make you feel more anxious and might interfere with the calming effects of your medication.

How Caffeine Affects Anxiety

Increased Anxiety: Caffeine can make you feel jittery or nervous. This can make anxiety symptoms worse.

Medication Interaction: Caffeine can change how your body processes anxiety medications. This might make the medication less effective.

What to Do

Limit Caffeine: Try to drink less coffee, tea, or soda while taking anxiety medication.

Talk to Your Doctor: If you are worried about caffeine and your medication, talk to your doctor.
