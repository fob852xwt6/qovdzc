Lyme disease is a bacterial infection caused by Borrelia bacteria, most commonly Borrelia burgdorferi in the United States. It spreads to humans through the bite of infected blacklegged ticks (deer ticks). Early symptoms often include a bullseye-shaped rash, fever, fatigue, headache, and joint pain. If left untreated, the infection can spread to the joints, heart, and nervous system.
