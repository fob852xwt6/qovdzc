What could cause joint pain in the left elbow?

Reviewed by Tomas Diaz, MD

Emergency Medicine

Left elbow pain commonly occurs due to tendon overuse (tendinopathy) and arthritis, though there are many other possible causes.

Elbow pain most commonly stems from tendon injury or arthritis. Tendinopathy occurs when the tendons around your elbow become overused or irritated, often from repetitive motions. Arthritis is another frequent cause, where joint inflammation leads to pain from wear and tear. That being said, there are many other possible causes of elbow pain. If you experience persistent or severe elbow pain, it's important to consult a healthcare provider for a proper evaluation of your specific condition.
