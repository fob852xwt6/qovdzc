Frequent urination in men can be associated with various conditions, and the presence of urethral pus often suggests an infection. One condition that can cause these symptoms is prostatitis, which is the inflammation of the prostate gland. Prostatitis can lead to urinary symptoms such as frequent urination and may also be accompanied by signs of infection, like urethral discharge or pus.

Prostatitis and Urinary Tract Infection: Prostatitis can present with symptoms like frequent urination and urethral discharge. This condition involves inflammation and sometimes infection of the prostate, which can irritate the urinary tract and lead to these symptoms.

If someone is experiencing these symptoms, it is important for them to seek medical advice for a proper diagnosis and treatment plan.
