Your body naturally makes vaginal discharge to clean out old cells and bad germs. Normally, this discharge is clear or white. When it turns yellow, it may mean that something is off balance in your vagina. Some infections, like bacterial vaginosis, can cause yellow or off-white discharge because the good and bad bacteria are not in balance. Other causes can include certain sexually transmitted infections, which may change the discharge’s color or thickness. Although a small change in color can sometimes be normal, yellow discharge that comes with other symptoms—such as itching, burning, or a strong smell—can mean your body is fighting an infection. It is important to notice if your body feels different or if you have any discomfort. If you’re worried or if the yellow discharge persists, it is a good idea to talk to a healthcare provider. They can examine you and run tests to see what is causing the change and help you get the right treatment. Taking care of any changes early can help keep you safe and healthy.
