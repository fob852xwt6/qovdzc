Explanation

Ankle pain that worsens at night while resting might be linked to Restless Legs Syndrome (RLS). RLS is a condition that causes uncomfortable sensations in the legs and a strong urge to move them, often occurring during periods of rest or inactivity, such as at night.

What is Restless Legs Syndrome?

Symptoms: RLS often involves tingling, burning, or aching feelings in the legs, which can make it hard to sleep.

When it Occurs: These symptoms are typically worse in the evening or night when you are trying to relax or sleep.

Connection to Other Conditions

Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD): Research suggests that RLS can occur in people with PAD, a condition that affects blood flow to the legs.

Differentiating from Cramps: It is important to differentiate RLS from nocturnal leg cramps, which are sudden, painful muscle contractions.
