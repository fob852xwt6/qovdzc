The pituitary gland makes TSH in your brain. When you have low thyroid levels, your pituitary gland responds by making more TSH. When your thyroid levels are high, your pituitary gland produces less TSH.

If your TSH levels are too high or too low, it could mean that your thyroid is not working correctly.

What are the benefits of a TSH test?

A TSH test can be used to diagnose thyroid problems. You should get a TSH test if you are having symptoms of low thyroid levels in your blood (hypothyroidism) or high thyroid levels in your blood (hyperthyroidism).

When will I need a TSH test?

Your healthcare provider will likely order a TSH test if they suspect you have hypothyroidism (underactive thyroid) or hyperthyroidism (overactive thyroid).
