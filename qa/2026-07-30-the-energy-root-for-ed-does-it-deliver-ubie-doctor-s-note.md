What Is Korean Red Ginseng?

Derived from the root of Panax ginseng C.A. Meyer.

"Red" refers to a steaming process that alters its chemical profile, boosting levels of active compounds called ginsenosides.

Long used in traditional East Asian medicine as an adaptogen to increase energy, resilience and sexual vitality.

How Might It Help Erectile Function?

Research suggests Korean red ginseng may support erections through several mechanisms:

Nitric Oxide (NO) Production• Ginsenosides can enhance NO synthesis in the endothelium (blood vessel lining), relaxing smooth muscle in the penis and improving blood flow.

Hormonal Modulation• Some studies indicate mild increases in testosterone levels or improvements in the testosterone-to-cortisol ratio.

Antioxidant & Anti-Inflammatory Effects• Reduces oxidative stress that can damage blood vessels and nerves essential for erection.

Neuromodulation• May improve nerve signal transmission required for an erection.

Key Clinical Trials

1. Jang et al. (2008)

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (Int J Impot Res).

Participants: 90 men with mild to moderate ED.

Intervention: 1,000 mg Korean red ginseng three times daily vs. placebo for 8 weeks.

Results:• Significant improvement in the International Index of Erectile Function (IIEF) scores in the ginseng group vs. placebo.• Increased satisfaction and ability to maintain erections.• Side effects were mild (e.g., headache, insomnia) and similar to placebo.

2. Yi et al. (2010)

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (BJU Int).

Participants: 60 men with mild to moderate ED.

Intervention: 900 mg Korean red ginseng three times daily vs. placebo for 12 weeks.

Results:• Statistically significant improvements in IIEF-Erectile Function domain and rigidity tests.• Improved frequency of satisfactory sexual intercourse.• No serious adverse events; a few reported gastrointestinal discomfort.

Bottom Line: Both well-designed trials showed modest yet clinically meaningful improvements in erectile function compared with placebo, with a favorable safety profile.

Adjust dose under medical advice if you experience side effects or on advice of your physician.
