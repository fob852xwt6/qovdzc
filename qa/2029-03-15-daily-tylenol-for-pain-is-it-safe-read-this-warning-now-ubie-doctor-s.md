Published on: 7/21/2026

Is it safe to take Tylenol every day for chronic pain?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Taking Tylenol (acetaminophen) every day for chronic pain is generally not considered safe without medical supervision. Long-term daily use has been linked to serious side effects, including liver damage, kidney problems, and increased cardiovascular risk. While occasional use at recommended doses is typically safe for most adults, consistent daily use—especially at higher doses or combined with alcohol—can lead to cumulative harm. If you rely on Tylenol daily to manage chronic pain, it's essential to speak with a healthcare provider to evaluate safer, more effective long-term treatment options.
