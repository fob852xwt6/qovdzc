Burping and nausea happening together can mean different things. One common cause is a digestive issue called dyspepsia, which often makes people feel uncomfortable in their stomach. This can happen when the stomach doesn't work the way it should, leading to symptoms like burping and feeling sick.

Sometimes, burping can also be a sign of a heart problem, like angina, which is when the heart doesn't get enough blood. This is less common but important to know because it needs medical attention.
