YCT-529 is an experimental male birth control pill that works by blocking the retinoic acid receptor-alpha (RAR-α), a key part of the retinoid signaling pathway essential for sperm production. By selectively inhibiting this pathway, YCT-529 temporarily halts sperm development without affecting testosterone or hormonal balance. Early studies show it is reversible, non-hormonal, and highly effective in preclinical trials, making it a promising step toward expanding male contraceptive options.
