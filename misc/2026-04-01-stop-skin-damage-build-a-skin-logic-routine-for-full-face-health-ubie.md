Holistic focus: Skin-logic pairs targeted treatments (like red light) with cleansing, hydration, sun protection and lifestyle habits.

Evidence-based: Clinical studies in journals such as Photomedicine and Laser Surgery show red (630–660 nm) and near-infrared (810–850 nm) light boosts collagen, calms redness and accelerates repair.

Non-invasive: Unlike lasers that burn, red light gently penetrates layers to support cell energy (ATP production) without damage.

5. Monitoring Progress and Staying Safe

Keep a skin journal• Note session dates, device settings and any reactions.• Photograph monthly under consistent lighting for comparison.

Adjust as needed• If your skin feels tight or irritated, scale back light frequency or moisturizer application.• If breakouts worsen, pause the light therapy and consult a professional.

Know contraindications• Photosensitive conditions or medications (e.g., certain antibiotics, retinoids) may increase risk.• Seek personalized guidance if you have a history of melanoma or active skin cancer.
