What is Neuromyelitis Optica (NMO)?

Neuromyelitis optica (NMO) is a rare condition in which the immune system damages the spinal cord and the optic nerves.

Typical Symptoms of Neuromyelitis Optica (NMO)

Diagnostic Questions for Neuromyelitis Optica (NMO)

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you been diagnosed with Multiple Sclerosis?

Do you have weakness or paralysis on one side of your body?

Have you experienced partial loss of vision?

Do you have pain all over your body?

Is it hard for you to distinguish subtle differences in color intensity or brightness?

Treatment of Neuromyelitis Optica (NMO)

There is no cure for neuromyelitis optica, but treatments may help ease symptoms, prevent future relapses, and slow down the condition's progression. Medications include steroids and immunosuppressant therapy. Rehabilitation techniques, such as physical therapy, may be helpful.
