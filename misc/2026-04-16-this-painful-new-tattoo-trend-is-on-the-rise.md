# ​This Painful New Tattoo Trend Is On the Rise

(And if you're interested in getting the perfect tattoo, here's what you need to do.) But there is a new tattoo placement that’s gaining steam, according to a popular Instagram hashtag. More than 7,000 users have posted photos of armpit tattoos, using a hashtag that doesn’t mince words: #armpittattoo. And according to the users themselves, they hurt like hell.
