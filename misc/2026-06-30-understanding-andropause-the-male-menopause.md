Testosterone Replacement Therapy (TRT)

Testosterone Replacement Therapy (TRT) involves supplying the body with testosterone to increase levels to a healthy range. Whether low testosterone is caused by true hypogonadism or is an effect of another health issue, TRT can replenish testosterone that isn’t being produced by the body. This can result in significant improvement in symptoms, boosting energy, increasing libido, and improving mental health. TRT can be administered in a number of ways, including oral medications, topical creams or gels, subcutaneous pellets, injections, nasal sprays, or transdermal patches.

While TRT is a popular treatment for andropause, it is not the right treatment for everyone. If you have any of the following conditions, be sure to discuss them with your doctor before beginning TRT:

Sleep apnea

Cardiac issues (such as congestive heart failure)

Issues with prostate or urinary tract

High risk for cancer, especially prostate or breast cancer

High red blood cell count

Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT)

Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) is a treatment generally assumed to be exclusive to women. However, this is not the case. Men with low T may have other hormone deficiencies that need to be addressed. If this is the case, a doctor may prescribe an HRT treatment involving multiple hormones in addition to testosterone. The benefits and risks of HRT can vary based on what hormones are involved and other factors involving your health.
