Explanation

Prostate cancer can be hard to notice at first because its symptoms are often subtle. Some of the early signs that might be overlooked include:

Frequent urination: Needing to go to the bathroom more often, especially at night, can be an early sign.

Difficulty urinating: This includes trouble starting or stopping the flow of urine.

[Prostate Cancer](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/5428312498.html)

Weak urine flow: A weaker stream of urine can indicate an issue.

These symptoms can be mistaken for other less serious conditions, like an enlarged prostate, which is common in older men. It is important to talk to a doctor if you notice these changes, as early detection of prostate cancer can lead to better outcomes. If you're experiencing any of these symptoms, you can start by taking a free symptom checker test to better understand what might be causing them. Remember, these signs do not always mean you have cancer, but it is good to check with a healthcare provider.
