REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) is diagnosed through a sleep study called a polysomnogram, performed by a sleep specialist. During the overnight test, sensors monitor brain activity, muscle tone, breathing, and heart rate to detect unusual movements, vocalizations, or behaviors occurring during REM sleep. This helps confirm whether muscle paralysis, which normally occurs in REM sleep, is absent, a hallmark of RBD.
