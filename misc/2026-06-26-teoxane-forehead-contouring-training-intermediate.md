About this lesson

Master forehead filler and contouring with step-by-step guidance on assessment and safe forehead augmentation using Teosyal® RHA 2 from the Teoxane portfolio.

This lesson focuses on supraperiosteal cannula technique to create natural forehead convexity, address flat temporal regions and supraorbital sulcus, and achieve balanced facial proportions using soft, moldable hyaluronic acid fillers for natural contour and smooth projection.
