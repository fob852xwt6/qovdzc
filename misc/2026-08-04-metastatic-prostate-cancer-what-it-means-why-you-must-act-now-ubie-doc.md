What does it mean when prostate cancer becomes metastatic?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Metastatic prostate cancer occurs when cancer cells spread beyond the prostate gland to other areas of the body, most commonly the bones, lymph nodes, liver, or lungs. This stage is also known as advanced or stage 4 prostate cancer. Common symptoms include bone pain, fatigue, unexplained weight loss, difficulty urinating, and swelling in the legs. Early detection and understanding your symptoms are critical for guiding treatment options and improving quality of life.
