What is the Best Wool for Winter Sweaters

When faced with a wintery chill, sheep's wool is just about the most dependable fabric you can wear. Humans have relied on it for millenia, and despite all the advancements in modern synthetic fabrics, wool is still the best fabric for winter sweaters. While fibers shorn from other animals like alpaca and goats are technically wool too, sheep's wool, which is almost always just called wool, is the top choice for affordability and durability.

Affordable and Durable: Sheep's wool is far more affordable than cashmere and alpaca because of its abundant supply. In fact, Australia, Iceland, Ireland, New Zealand, and Scotland all have more sheep than people. While the durability of sheep’s wool is comparable to alpaca, it is far more durable than cashmere.

Blended vs 100% Sheep’s wool: The two major benefits of blending sheep’s wool with other fibers like cotton and polyester are a softer fabric and more affordable sweater. However, the tradeoff for that softness and affordability is a loss in durability and warmth. Going with 100% wool will provide a warmer sweater that will hold up to more wear and tear.

Wool vs Lambswool: Both wool and lambswool come from sheep, but lambswool is taken from younger sheep. Baby sheep produce much more delicate wool that makes for softer garments. The feel is closer to cashmere but without the fragility. Lambswool is usually much more expensive because baby sheep produce far less wool so there is less of it.
