# The Secret to Weighted Pushups

Weighted pushups are one of the ultimate badass exercises, up there with one-arm pullups and “anything Jason Statham does.”

The problem: “People usually struggle at the bottom position trying to push themselves up with a weight because you have less leverage there,” says Jeremy Frisch, U.S.A.W., owner and director of Achieve Performance Training in Clinton, Massachusetts, and creator of the 24-Hour Arms Workout. So once his athletes have mastered regular pushups, Frisch doesn’t progress them straight to weighted pushups. Instead, he has them grab an elastic exercise band and do band-resisted pushups. Watch the video below to see how it’s done:

Exercise bands provide the most resistance when they’re stretched the farthest. That’s perfect for building pushup strength, since it adds the most tension where you’re strongest—at the top of the pushup—while still providing some resistance at your relatively weaker bottom position. “It’s a great way to segue into doing weighted pushups," Frisch says. “Guys love it, too, because your chest and triceps are just pumped up after you do a few sets of those.”
