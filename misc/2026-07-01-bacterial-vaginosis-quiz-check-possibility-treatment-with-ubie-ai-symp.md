What is Bacterial Vaginosis?

A healthy human vagina contains various types of bacteria. When the balance of bacteria is disrupted, some types can overgrow and cause symptoms. Common triggers include vaginal washing, sexual intercourse, or use of an Intrauterine Device.

Typical Symptoms of Bacterial Vaginosis

Increased vaginal discharge that is smelly

Increase in vaginal discharge

Abnormal color, amount or odor of vaginal discharge

IUCD / coil use within the last 1 year

Soiled gential area e.g. due to diarrhea or proloned tampon use

Mental stress and physical fatigue

Fever

Been sexually active with multiple partners during the past 1 month
