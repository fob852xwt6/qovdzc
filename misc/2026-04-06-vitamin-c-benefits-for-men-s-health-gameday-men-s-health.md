Injections provide a more powerful dose of Vitamin C since they are delivered directly into the bloodstream. This bypasses the digestive system, providing you with a more immediate boost and ensuring maximum absorption by your body.

How often should I receive Vitamin C injections?

The frequency of Vitamin C injections will depend on your specific health needs and will be determined during your consultation with your Gameday healthcare provider.

Are there any side effects of Vitamin C injections?

Some possible side effects include discomfort at the injection site, fatigue, headaches, or, in rare cases, allergic reactions.

Who should consider Vitamin C injections?

Vitamin C injections might be the solution you need if you feel consistently fatigued, have a cold and want to shorten its duration, notice you are becoming sick frequently, have a Vitamin C deficiency, or have been feeling stressed.

Can Vitamin C injections cure colds?

While Vitamin C injections can help reduce the duration and severity of cold symptoms, they do not cure the common cold.

How quickly do Vitamin C injections work?

Most individuals notice the effects of Vitamin C injections within a few weeks of receiving them. Some might notice the effects within a few hours after administration, although the exact time varies.
