What is a B12 shot?

A vitamin B12 shot, also known as a cobalamin injection, is one of several ways you can receive vital vitamin B12. This plays an important role in maintaining nerve and blood cells, DNA synthesis, and red blood cell production. It’s also essential to maintain a healthy metabolism, regulate your mood, and support your immune system.

The downside of oral supplementation is that it’s difficult for your body to absorb vitamin B12 through your stomach. As a result, you may struggle with symptoms of low levels of vitamin B12, such as fatigue, headaches, and even heart palpitations. Consider getting an infusion instead to make sure your body absorbs as much vitamin B12 as possible.

What are the benefits of B12 shots?

Have you ever wondered what is involved in getting an injection of vitamin B12? Perhaps you’ve heard that shots are better than oral supplements but don’t know why. In short, injections have several benefits over oral supplementation. First and foremost, they bypass your digestive system and go straight into your bloodstream—where your body can more easily absorb them.

Aside from providing direct delivery, injections of vitamin B12 also have several other health benefits, including increased energy levels, reduced fatigue, improved mood and cognitive function, boosted metabolism, and reduction of homocysteine levels (which may help reduce your risk for heart disease).
