Turmeric is a spice that contains an active ingredient called curcumin, which might help with weight loss. Studies show that curcumin can help reduce the amount of fat in the body.

Fat Reduction: Research indicates that curcumin may reduce fat accumulation. In studies with certain nematodes, curcumin was shown to decrease fat storage.

Body Processes: Curcumin might influence certain body processes that are involved in managing weight. This includes affecting metabolism and reducing inflammation, which can help with weight control.
