Frequently Asked Questions

Intravenous (IV) iron therapy delivers iron directly into your bloodstream. As a result, absorption is faster and more efficient than with traditional oral iron supplementation. At Drip Hydration, the medical professionals administering your iron IV therapy use Venofer (iron sucrose), which supports efficient iron absorption. You do not have to visit an office; our licensed nurses will come directly to your home or office.

What are the benefits of Iron IV therapy?

Low iron levels are linked to mental fog, persistent fatigue, and shortness of breath. The benefits of iron IV therapy include meaningful improvements in your overall energy levels, oxygen transport, cognitive clarity, and immune system function. Additionally, iron IV therapy is associated with healthier hair and stronger nails. And, because IV delivery is more efficient than oral supplementation, you may notice improvements more quickly than with oral supplementation.

What can Iron IV therapy help with?

Enjoying a convenient IV iron therapy treatment in the comfort of your home, office, or hotel room takes the stress out of correcting deficient iron levels. Our licensed medical professionals expertly administer iron via IV therapy, which helps replenish iron levels associated with anemia and iron deficiency.

How many Iron IV sessions will I need?

Each client has individual needs, and we can customize an iron IV regimen to meet your unique needs. There is no guesswork involved. The number of iron IV sessions you might schedule depends on your overall wellness goals, recent lab results, and medical provider recommendations. Because the goal is to restore optimum iron levels, we work with you to determine the ideal number of IV sessions.

Does Iron IV therapy have side effects?

Our clients typically tolerate iron IV therapy well and do not experience severe side effects. If they do appear, they tend to be minor, such as soreness at the injection site, a mild fever, and lightheadedness. If you are concerned about more serious symptoms, remember that licensed medical professionals will be at your side throughout the infusion therapy, monitoring your well-being. Moreover, the Drip Hydration team will help you prepare for your iron IV session to minimize side effects. For example, drinking plenty of fluids before treatment will help reduce the risk of dizziness.

How often should I do Iron IV therapy?

With your wellness goals and the severity of your iron deficiency as the driving factors, treatment frequency varies from client to client. Is Iron IV safe in pregnancy?

If you are pregnant or suspect you might be, consult your OB-GYN first. IV iron therapy may be recommended during pregnancy when medically appropriate.
