What is Acute Encephalopathy?

Acute encephalopathy refers to sudden brain dysfunction, which can be caused by various factors such as infection or toxins.

Treatment of Acute Encephalopathy

[with Ubie AI](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/)

Acute encephalopathy treatment depends on the underlying cause but generally includes supportive care, such as monitoring vital signs, providing fluids, and managing symptoms. Specific treatments may target the underlying issue, such as infection or metabolic disturbances.
