What can be done to quickly stop diarrhea while at work?

Internal Medicine

Loperamide is a widely used over-the-counter medication that quickly slows down bowel movements, making it an effective option for managing sudden diarrhea symptoms during the workday. It typically starts working within an hour, helping you regain control and continue your daily activities with minimal disruption.

However, diarrhea can stem from many causes—ranging from mild dietary issues to infections or underlying conditions that need proper attention. Simply masking the symptom may not address the root problem. Before reaching for medication, it's worth understanding what's actually causing your discomfort.
