# This Quick Single-Kettlebell Workout Burns Fat and Torches Your Whole Body

In a recent YouTube video, kettlebell workout master Eric Leija demonstrated a fat-burning workout that incorporates simple bodyweight exercises and more advanced kettlebell flow moves to hit every part of the body.

[Burns Fat](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/6045045545.html)

First things first, Leija demonstrates a 2-round warmup, going from child's pose to a kneeling mountain climber position 5 times on each side. Then, starting with the bottom of the bell pressed to his chest and does a loaded "good morning" hinge, then grabbing the handle of the bell and going into a horned squat. He repeats this for 10 reps. "Keep a soft bit on the knees in that hinge," he says. "I'm not straight-legged here, I'm putting the tension on the hamstrings. From there, Leija goes straight into a set of 5 squat jumps with pause, holding the position at the lower end of the movement, followed by 10 jumping jacks. He rests for 30 seconds, then repeats the whole warmup.

The workout proper starts with 5 reps on each side of the kettlebell waiter racked squat, holding the bell in one hand and supporting the handle with the other. "Slow and controlled, keep that chest upright," he says. Next he goes from a plank to a mountain climber position, this time with a reach, performing 5 reps on each side. After a 60 second rest, repeat these sets a total of 4 times.

Leija's next set starts with a combo move: kettlebell row to cheat clean. "Use your other hand to support the clean and guide the bell around your wrist," he advises. He performs this for 5 reps on each side, then goes straight into 10 pushups. Again, all of this is to be completed 4 times with 60 seconds of rest in between.

The finishing round starts with a 3-move flow: cheat clean to squat to press, switching sides on each repetition for a total of 10. The second flow is a kettlebell pull into situp, performed for 10 reps: the key to this one is being as slow and controlled as possible to engage the core while lowering out of the situp. "The slower you go on the way down, the more you're going to light up those abs," says Leija. As this set is more intense, Leija recommends doing it a total of 3 times, keeping that 60-second rest in between.
