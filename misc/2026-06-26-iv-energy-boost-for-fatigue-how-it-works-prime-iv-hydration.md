What Is Fatigue and Its Causes?

It’s common to have occasional fatigue. For instance, you can experience fatigue and exhaustion after a strenuous workout at your neighborhood gym or a long day at the office. But after sleeping, you typically feel revived and energized. Even though you got a good night’s sleep and took it easy, there are times when your tiredness can linger. This is tiredness. If you’re exhausted, your tiredness could persist even after you’ve rested. Lethargy and a lack of motivation are further examples of it. But what causes it? There are several things that can be behind your feelings of fatigue, including working long days. Over-exerting yourself at the gym can also contribute, and there are clinical causes of fatigue too. A few common causes of clinical fatigue include anemia, allergies, flu, fibromyalgia, depression, arthritis, heart disease, and sleep apnea. All of these conditions stop your body from operating at its full potential. If you suffer from one of these ailments, you could believe that you lack the energy to function normally.
