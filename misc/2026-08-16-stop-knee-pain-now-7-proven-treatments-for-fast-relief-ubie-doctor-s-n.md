Explanation

There are many ways to help a sore knee feel better. One common method is to use medicines like ibuprofen or acetaminophen. These help lower pain and swelling. Another option is physical therapy. A physical therapist can show you fun exercises to make the muscles around the knee stronger. Health experts also suggest using stretching and gentle workouts like walking or swimming. Sometimes, doctors may use injection treatments. Corticosteroid injections can help reduce pain, and other injections may help the knee move better. In cases where the pain is long-lasting and other treatments do not work well, surgery might be needed. This can include procedures to fix or replace part of the knee. For people with knee osteoarthritis, there are special slow-acting medicines that may help the knee last longer. Choosing a treatment depends on the pain, how the knee works, and the overall health of the person.
