What is Amyloidosis?

A disease in which "amyloid," an abnormal type of protein, accumulates in various organs, causing damage. Affected areas can include the heart, kidneys, and gastrointestinal tract. The causes are diverse.

Typical Symptoms of Amyloidosis

Whole body pain

Frequent stomach or abdominal pain with unknown causes

Blood in stool

Wheezing or whistling sounds when breathing

Numbness in the fingers

Fainting

Dizziness

Warmth in the joints

Diagnostic Questions for Amyloidosis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have pain all over your body?

Do you have frequent stomach or abdominal pain with unknown causes?

Have you noticed any blood in your stool?

Do you experience wheezing or whistling sounds when breathing?

Do you experience numbness in your fingers?

Treatment of Amyloidosis

There is currently no cure for amyloidosis, so treatment focuses on slowing the disease and managing symptoms. As organs become damaged, treatments like dialysis or organ transplants may be needed.
