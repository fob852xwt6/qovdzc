Chris Evans: Shared super rare video of him performing Captain America stunts

Chris Evans has said he’s ready to hang up his Captain America shield, but it looks like he’s already getting nostalgic for his time as an Avenger.

“In honor of Infinity War coming out next week, here are a few videos I found from 2013 while prepping for The Winter Soldier,” Evans tweeted on Thursday alongside a video showing off his fight training for the film.

Truly, Evans has dedicated years to honing his Captain America physique. As he shared with Men’s Health way back in 2011, it all came down to training with Simon Waterson, and working out for two hours a day for months.

Chris Hemsworth: Made his body double work overtime

Sure, Chris Hemsworth may be the face of Thor, but it’s easy to forget there’s another man behind that cape: His stunt double, Bobby Holland Hanton.

"Thor is definitely the most difficult character that we have to get in shape for," Hanton told news.com.au on Thursday. "Chris is already huge. He's much bigger than me naturally, so I have to train twice a day."

According to Hanton, in order to maintain his Hemsworth look alike body he’s had to do two-a-days in the gym and live by a restrictive diet for more than a year and a half.

“It’s a job in itself,” Hanton said. “We might train first thing in the morning and last thing at night. We try and eat every two-and-a-half hours. For breakfast we might have something like scrambled eggs with steak and spinach. No carbs in the morning. Then two hours later we might have a chicken breast and salad. After our first training session we’d introduce the carbs, usually brown rice, sweet potato or quinoa and then we’d continue to eat carbs up until the last meal. It’s pretty intense.”

Meanwhile, while Hanton is training Hemsworth has been busy Instagramming up a storm from his various vacations and chillen with Usain Bolt. So, seems fair.
