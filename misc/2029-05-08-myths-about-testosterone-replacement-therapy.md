Long-term health implications

Potential long-term effects include:

Hormonal imbalance: Prolonged use of TRT can disrupt the body’s natural hormone production, making it difficult to stop therapy without experiencing significant drops in testosterone levels.

Infertility: TRT can reduce sperm production, potentially leading to infertility, which can be a major consideration for those wishing to start or expand their families.

Liver Health: Oral testosterone can have adverse effects on liver function, though this is less of a concern with non-oral administration methods like injections or transdermal patches.

Bone Health: While testosterone can support bone density, improper management of TRT may lead to bone health issues over time.
