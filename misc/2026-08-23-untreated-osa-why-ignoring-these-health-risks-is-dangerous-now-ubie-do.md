How serious are the health risks of untreated OSA?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) is a condition where breathing repeatedly stops and starts during sleep. When left untreated, OSA can lead to serious health complications, including high blood pressure, heart disease, stroke, type 2 diabetes, and chronic daytime fatigue that increases accident risk.

Because OSA symptoms—like loud snoring, gasping during sleep, or morning headaches—often overlap with other conditions, identifying the cause early is critical.
