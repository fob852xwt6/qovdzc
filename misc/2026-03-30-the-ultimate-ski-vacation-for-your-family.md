# The Ultimate Ski Vacation for Your Family

Imagine that you've just carved an epic line down the mountain, punctuated with a hockey stop that sends a wall of powder spraying over an enraptured crowd that's been frozen in place, watching your run. As you lift your goggles to salute your adoring fans, you feel a tap on the shoulder. You turn and see the local pro, extending a hand. "Sir," he says. "This is the bunny hill."

Joking aside, it can be tough trying to balance family ski time with the temptation of double black diamonds further up the mountain. And it can feel even tougher to find that unicorn of a destination that offers great snow, a faraway feel, on- and off-mountain activities for everyone, and kid-friendly vibes that don't dumb down the entire experience.

[The Ultimate Ski](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/blog/5182395850.html)

Enter: Big White Ski Resort, which has been voted Canada’s most family-friendly. It’s got world-class ski conditions, a walkable village, breweries—a veritable playground of diverse terrain with just about every activity imaginable, all with a reduced price tag. (Shoutout to the Canadian dollar!)

It’s the type of trip that might inspire you to take even more vacations with the kids. Really! Here’s why.

The kids will be taken care of

Big White’s Kids’ Centre is the type of place your kids are going to want to go, whether it's to ski, skate, snowshoe, do arts and crafts, or refine their palates with the centre's in-house chefs.

And of course, there's ski school—no longer dreaded at Big White. They've got a slew of age-appropriate outings and group lessons to choose from designed to build self confidence and keep things light and fun. There's also a "valet" service, where an instructor will actually pick your kids up and drop them off once the day is done. That's a huge thing off your plate, and lets everyone enjoy the mountain on their own schedule.

There's plenty for you to enjoy

Big White isn’t the kind of resort that confines adults to blue squares or green circles. While the kids are out, you’ll find multiple double black diamonds, bowls, and killer views of the Monashee Mountains from the mountain's summit.

If you want to hit the village later on, the resort has an online booking service for vetted babysitters so you can enjoy an evening sans young'uns.

Everything is walkable

The mere thought of wrangling with car seats, snowsuits, and assorted gear can be enough to make you forgo a day or evening out. But Big White just so happens to be Canada’s largest completely ski-in, ski-out village. That means you can park your car when you arrive and not need it again until you leave. You can ski to your hotel, one of the more than 20 restaurants in town, or the on-mountain spa. If you can’t ski it, you can walk it—and the kids can burn off some extra energy.

The restaurants appeal to everyone

You shouldn't have to give up Instagram-worthy meals for a week just because you have the kids in tow. (If you want to eat nothing but chicken fingers and fries, that should be your choice.) Big White's eateries have the whole family covered.

Elevation 57 is Canada's highest brewery at 5,757 feet above sea level, which hastens the effects of the 12 craft brews on tap. They also offer private tours—the perfect opportunity to book one of those babysitters. Gastropubs in town have kid-friendly menus as well as multiple daily happy hours. Tapas cafés feature globally inspired small plates alongside tabletop fire pits for marshmallow roasting.

You don't have to ski to have fun

If little bodies tire of the mountain early or you just want something else to do, Big White will make sure you're not looking long. The resort offers horse-drawn sleigh rides through Canada’s backcountry, snowmobile tours through powder (with a track for mini-snowmobiles, driven by mini-humans), dogsledding, tubing, ice-climbing, and skating at the country’s highest-elevation rink.

In fact, the resort hosts a family-friendly event every night of the week, culminating with Saturday-night fireworks displays over the village. And because you deserve a few minutes to yourself after all that, the Elevation Spa offers deep-tissue massages using organic products that promise to fast-track recovery from the slopes, the beers, or, let's be honest, the kids.
