# 24 Stylish T-Shirts for Under $25

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

Every man needs a solid set of basics in his wardrobe— pieces that pair with anything, and won’t go out of style for years— and that includes a good t-shirt. But the key is finding ones that look stylish, no matter your budget.

Fortunately for your wallet, many brands now offer affordable options that you’ll actually want to wear on their own. “Inexpensive t-shirts don't necessarily read as lower quality, and they can be styled alongside the more expensive brands,” says Samantha Brown, celebrity stylist. “The most important component is fit, since an ill-fitting t-shirt will never look expensive.”

Fit and proportion are key. Make sure the chest, shoulders, and stomach all fit well (in other words: not too tight, not too loose). And if you want that tee to stay perfectly fitted, let 100 percent cotton t-shirts air dry.

When looking for your new favorite tee, don’t mistake an undershirt for a t-shirt (and save v-necks for layering under dress shirts, too). As t-shirt trends and styles change, it can be tricky to decipher between the two. “Traditionally, an undershirt is made of light cotton and is slightly longer than a t-shirt because it's meant to be tucked in beneath a button-up shirt,” Brown says. “Long t-shirt lengths are trending, so it is difficult to differentiate between modern t-shirts and standard undershirts. Many men favor thin undershirts to avoid overheating or adding bulk, whereas thicker t-shirts provide more coverage when worn alone.” (Pro tip: If it comes in a pack of three, it’s probably an undershirt.)

The details—pockets vs. no pockets—are up to you and what fits your individual style. (Pocket tees, for what it's worth, look great on their own.) But whatever direction you choose, as long as you like it, you can't go wrong. Here, we’ve rounded up some great options, all under $25.
