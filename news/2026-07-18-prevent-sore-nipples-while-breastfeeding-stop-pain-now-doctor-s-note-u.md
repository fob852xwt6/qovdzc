How can I prevent sore nipples while breastfeeding?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

[Pain Now Doctor](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/2829210389.html)

Sore nipples during breastfeeding are often caused by an improper latch. To prevent discomfort, make sure your baby's mouth covers most of the areola—not just the nipple—and that their lips are flanged outward. Applying a lanolin-based or hydrogel nipple cream between feedings can soothe irritation and support healing. If pain persists, consult a lactation specialist to rule out issues like tongue-tie or infection.

Because sore nipples can sometimes signal a deeper issue—such as thrush, mastitis, or a latch problem needing hands-on correction—it's worth understanding your symptoms before they worsen. Take a free, instant, online symptom check to clarify what's happening and confidently plan your next steps.
