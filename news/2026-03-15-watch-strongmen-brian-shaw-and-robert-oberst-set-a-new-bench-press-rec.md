# Watch Strongmen Brian Shaw and Robert Oberst Set a New Bench Press 'Record' at Planet Fitness

Strongmen Brian Shaw and Robert Oberst recently trained together in an actual gym. A Planet Fitness to be exact, and the results were record-breaking and hilarious. The guys met up to break a bench press record at the famously weight-conscious gym (you've probably heard, or maybe even experienced, the infamous Lunk Alarm).

"The discussion was, what is the heaviest bench press that's ever been done at Planet Fitness?" says Shaw. "And we couldn't come up with a number."

"I don't think it's too high. I would be surprised if it was even 315, honestly," says Oberst.

The guys discuss that since they don't have bench presses in Planet Fitness, they'll have to use a Smith machine instead.

The guys plan to beat 315 pounds "in the warmup", and plan to set a heaviest bench press record.

"Let's get to work," says Shaw, with the guys changing into Planet Fitness "Be Free" shirts to put in the work.

They warm up on the Smith machine, quickly adding on weight. And Oberst snacks on pizza as Shaw finishes up a set.

"Where the hell did you get a pizza from?" says Shaw.

"I'm a new member!" says Oberst, mouth full of cheesy goodness.

"So because it's my second time here I don't get one?" says Shaw.

"Yeah, I'm better than you. When I'm done, if there's any left, I'll let you look at it," says Oberst.

Then, the guys get back to training.

"It's weird doing it on a Smith machine, honestly," says Oberst.

"It's also weird trying not to be too loud," laughs Shaw. "Cuz normally I would be going nuts!"

Oberst taps out at five plates on each side, while Shaw pushes it with six plates for 560 pounds.

"Eat your pizza, and let me go to work," says Shaw to Oberst.

He knocks out the rep, and the guys discuss the record.

"If anybody else has done more than that at a Planet Fitness, show us," says Oberst. "We need a plaque and a pizza."

Workout complete, they talk about how they plan to recover.
