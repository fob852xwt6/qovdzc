Acetaminophen may raise blood pressure in some people, potentially impacting heart health. While often considered safer than NSAIDs for those with cardiovascular concerns, recent studies show regular use can elevate systolic blood pressure, especially in people with hypertension. Several factors influence this risk, including dosage, frequency of use, and individual health conditions.
